Philippine Contract & Consumer Law Notice (Civil Code Arts. 1482, 1582–1599)
• Default Rule (Simultaneous Exchange): Standard catalog orders without partial terms are payable at the exact time and place of delivery or pickup.
• Custom Orders & Reservations: For custom-fabricated goods or high-value orders, an agreed down payment or half-payment serves as earnest money (Art. 1482) to allocate materials and schedule fabrication. Production begins upon confirmation of the initial deposit.
• Delivery Obligation: Once the agreed initial payment is verified, the seller is legally bound to fulfill and deliver the product according to the agreed specifications and timeframe.
