Payment

  • Pay online through our secure payment partner (Xendit). We never see or store your card details.
  • Cash on delivery / cash on pickup is available. You pay when you receive or collect your order.

Your order is reserved and stock is set aside once we confirm payment. Prices are in Philippine pesos and may change without notice, but the price on a confirmed order does not.

Cancellations

You can ask to cancel an order from My Orders while it is still Pending confirmation or Paid. Our team reviews the request and emails you the result.

Once an order is Packed, or on its way, it can no longer be cancelled. After you receive it, you can request a refund instead (see below).

If you already paid online and the cancellation is approved, the amount goes back to your original payment method.

Refunds

After your order is Delivered or Picked up, you can request a refund from My Orders within 7 days.

Reasons we accept

  • Damaged or defective item
  • Wrong item received
  • Item not as described
  • Missing items
  • Other problems you describe

What happens next

  • Our team reviews the request and may ask for photos or the item back.
  • If approved, online payments go back to the original payment method. For cash orders, we contact you to hand back the amount.
  • We may refund part of the amount when only some items are affected.
  • Only one refund request can be made per order.

Privacy

We handle your personal information in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173).

What we collect

Your name, email, mobile number, delivery addresses, your orders, and the payment status of each order. Online card or e-wallet details are entered on our payment partner’s page, not ours.

Why we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your orders.
  • To send you emails about your orders and account.
  • To keep records our business needs, such as invoices and stock history.

Who sees it

Only our authorized staff and the services we use to run the shop (such as payment and email providers). We do not sell your information.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it. You can edit your profile and addresses in the shop. For anything else, contact us below.
