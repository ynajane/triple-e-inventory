Help & Policies

How do I place an order?

Open the online shop, add products to your cart, then check out and choose delivery or pickup. You may be asked to sign in or create an account first.

Can I pick up my order instead of delivery?

Yes. Choose pickup at checkout. For how delivery and pickup work, see Delivery & pickup on the Help page.

Do you make custom or made-to-size items?

Yes. Send a quote request from the shop, and our team will reply with a quote you can review before going ahead.

What about payments, cancellations and refunds?

These are explained in plain language on our Help & Policies page, including what we accept and what happens next.

How is my personal information used?

Our Privacy section describes what we collect, why we use it, who sees it and your rights. You can read it any time on the Help page.
